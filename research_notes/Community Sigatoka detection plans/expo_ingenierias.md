## Q1. What is Expo Ingenierías: campuses, timing, categories, registration, format, and how it is evaluated?

### Takeaway
Expo Ingenierías is the end-of-semester project showcase and contest run by each region or campus of the Escuela de Ingeniería y Ciencias (EIC). The best-documented edition is Región Monterrey's 28th, held in Aug–Dec 2026. It has 3 categories: research/improvement proposal, physical prototype and software prototype. The process runs abstract preselection, then a video plus technical report, then a virtual round, then a one-day in-person round on **1 December 2026**. Two optional attributes, Entrepreneurship and Social Impact, are judged by separate juries with published rubrics. **Monterrey registration closed on 23 October 2026.** A team that did not register is out of the Monterrey AD2026 contest. I found no public 2026 call for Guadalajara or Región Occidente.

### Cited Findings

Its stated goals are to bring students and professors closer to industry leaders and to promote challenging projects that apply professional competencies. — [EI-MTY home](https://expoingenierias-mty.github.io/pagina-inicio/)

  - Software: "Trismegisto: asistente de diagnóstico por IA para detectar síndrome aórtico agudo en TAC".

**Evaluation: the published criteria (Annex 1, academic jury)**
- **Physical prototype** expected result: "Prototipo funcional de máquina, mecanismo, dispositivo, artefacto o similar". Evidence: technical report and video. Criteria (verbatim questions):
  - "¿Qué desarrollaste en tu proyecto este semestre? Si es un proyecto de semestres anteriores, ¿qué le aportaste de nuevo?"
  - "¿Qué tan creativa fue tu aportación?"
  - "¿El funcionamiento del prototipo es apropiado y demuestra la solución al problema?"
  - "¿Se aprecia que el prototipo resiste y funciona consistentemente?"
  - "¿Qué técnicas, tecnología o herramientas de ingeniería o ciencia aplicaron?"
  - "¿En qué medida la solución del problema tiene un impacto en la industria o en la sociedad?"
  — [Conv28](https://expoingenierias-mty.github.io/pagina-inicio/documentos/CONVOCATORIA%2028ed.pdf)
- **Software prototype** criteria add: "¿El software desarrollado funcionó y demuestra la solución al problema? ¿El software es práctico, entendible y fácil de usar?" — [Conv28](https://expoingenierias-mty.github.io/pagina-inicio/documentos/CONVOCATORIA%2028ed.pdf)
- **Research/improvement** criteria add: justification "en términos operativos, técnicos y/o económicos", replicability in another context, and theoretical framework. — [Conv28](https://expoingenierias-mty.github.io/pagina-inicio/documentos/CONVOCATORIA%2028ed.pdf)
- The academic rubric's **point weights are not published**. Only the guiding questions above are public. The attribute rubrics and the abstract-preselection rubric do have explicit scoring (below). — [Conv28](https://expoingenierias-mty.github.io/pagina-inicio/documentos/CONVOCATORIA%2028ed.pdf)

**Abstract preselection rubric (Annex 9)**, scored 0/1/2 per criterion by EIC professors and the organizing committee:
- **SMART objective**, max 200 characters, built around a verifiable result. An objective that is just an activity ("investigar", "conocer") scores 0.
- **Context/problem** with data that sizes the problem.
- **Methodology**, specific enough that an evaluator "no tenga que hacer suposiciones".
- **Expected results/deliverables**, verifiable and tied to the academic period.
- **Resources**, with an explicit judgment of whether they are sufficient.
- **Relevance and impact**, with "magnitud, indicador, cambio esperado o evidencia verificable". Generic claims like "ayudará al medio ambiente" score 0.
- The abstract is at most 250 words.
— [Conv28](https://expoingenierias-mty.github.io/pagina-inicio/documentos/CONVOCATORIA%2028ed.pdf)

**Required deliverables (Annexes 4–6)**
- **Video:** MP4, **3–5 minutes**, filename at most 30 characters.
  - It "no puede estar generado completamente con IA", must have an audible explanation, and must include images or clips of tests.
  - It cannot be only a demo with no explanation, or only explanation with no project.
  - Required content: team, professors and course; problem; what the project does and how; why it is innovative; scope; techniques with figures, diagrams or charts; results.
  - For a physical prototype, also: physical, technical and design description, and "Demuestra que tu prototipo funciona de manera robusta y consistente —el mismo desempeño siempre—, de preferencia sobre fondo blanco".
  - Videos that break these rules are dropped from the showcase.
  — [Conv28](https://expoingenierias-mty.github.io/pagina-inicio/documentos/CONVOCATORIA%2028ed.pdf)
- **Technical report:** PDF (filename at most 30 characters). It can be the course deliverable. Recommended sections include problem statement, justification, theoretical framework, hypothesis, methodology, engineering techniques, resources, results, conclusions and bibliography. Projects entering Social Impact or Entrepreneurship must include "el resultado del impacto del proyecto". — [Conv28](https://expoingenierias-mty.github.io/pagina-inicio/documentos/CONVOCATORIA%2028ed.pdf)
- **Project profile:** 3 representative images (image 1 ideally an animated GIF), a background image and an optional logo. **Uploading fewer than 3 images, team-member photos, or images in PDF format is grounds for disqualification.** — [Conv28](https://expoingenierias-mty.github.io/pagina-inicio/documentos/CONVOCATORIA%2028ed.pdf)

  — [Conecta Tec GDL, Dec 2020](https://conecta.tec.mx/es/noticias/guadalajara/educacion/asi-es-expo-ingenierias-del-tec-soluciones-innovadoras-y)

### Inferences
- For the drone plus on-board AI system, the best fit is **"Desarrollo de prototipo físico"**. That category rewards a working, robust and consistent prototype, which matches the drone and on-board inference hardware. The strategic area that best fits a smallholder-community framing is **"Cambio Climático y Sustentabilidad"**, since the call names "seguridad alimentaria" and "equidad social". "Transformación Industrial" (AI, cyber-physical systems) is the alternative if the pitch stays technology-led.
- The academic questions put heavy weight on **what is new this semester** ("¿qué le aportaste de nuevo?"). If the drone and detector were built in earlier periods, the refocus on low-resource communities needs to show new technical work this semester: field validation, cost reduction, offline usability features or local-language UI. Repackaging alone will not answer that question.
- If the team is at Monterrey and registered before 23 Oct, today (8 Nov) is the day before preselection results. The **critical upcoming deadline is 20 Nov 2026** for the video, report and profile. If they are at Guadalajara or another campus, the regional call must be confirmed directly (see Gaps).
- The rule "funciona de manera robusta y consistente — el mismo desempeño siempre" points to showing repeated trials (for example, the same leaf set run N times with identical outputs, or several flights) in the video and at the stand.

- **No 2026 call found for Guadalajara / Región Occidente** or for any campus other than Monterrey. The team's campus is not stated in the repo (PRODUCT.md only says "Platanos Machos, MR3002B, Tec"). Confirm with the course professor which regional Expo the team falls under and its AD2026 dates.
- Point weights for the academic jury's rubric (Annex 1) are not published, only guiding questions.

## Q2. Who are the judges and what awards exist?

### Takeaway
There are three juries, at least in Monterrey. The academic jury is EIC professors and experts in the area, and it scores every project virtually and in person. The Entrepreneurship jury comes from the ecosystem of the Instituto de Emprendimiento Eugenio Garza Lagüera (IEEGL). The Social Impact jury is made up of socioformador organizations linked to the Dirección de Servicio Social (DSS). Awards: top 3 per category, plus a prize in kind for 1st place. The Entrepreneurship and Social Impact tracks each recognize a top 3 and come with follow-up support. Both attribute juries judge **only in person** and reuse the academic technical score as 2 of their 10 points.

### Cited Findings
- Abstract preselection is done by "un comité formado por profesores de la EIC y los miembros del comité organizador". — [Conv28](https://expoingenierias-mty.github.io/pagina-inicio/documentos/CONVOCATORIA%2028ed.pdf)
- "Todos los proyectos que se expongan serán evaluados por docentes y expertos en el área." Virtual evaluators score through the platform. — [Conv27](https://expoingenierias-mty.github.io/pagina-inicio/documentos/CONVOCATORIA%2027ed.pdf); [Conv28](https://expoingenierias-mty.github.io/pagina-inicio/documentos/CONVOCATORIA%2028ed.pdf)
- **Entrepreneurship jury:** "representantes del ecosistema emprendedor y/o especialistas en innovación y emprendimiento invitados por el Instituto de Emprendimiento Eugenio Garza Lagüera". It evaluates "únicamente en formato presencial durante la Muestra". — [Conv28](https://expoingenierias-mty.github.io/pagina-inicio/documentos/CONVOCATORIA%2028ed.pdf)
- **Social Impact jury:** "representantes de organizaciones socio formadoras de servicio social que colaboran con la Dirección de Servicio Social en las unidades de formación con sentido humano". It also evaluates in person only. — [Conv28](https://expoingenierias-mty.github.io/pagina-inicio/documentos/CONVOCATORIA%2028ed.pdf)
- **Awards, 28th edition:**
  - Profesional: "reconocimiento al top 3 por categoría; y premio en especie al 1er lugar de cada una".
  - Posgrado: recognition and a prize in kind for the best project.
  - Emprendimiento: "reconocimiento del Instituto de Emprendimiento Garza Lagüera al top 3 y premio especial al 1er lugar".
  - Impacto Social: "reconocimiento al top 3 y, acompañamiento de la Dirección de Servicio Social para implementar los proyectos".
  - Finalist videos are shared on social media.
  — [Conv28](https://expoingenierias-mty.github.io/pagina-inicio/documentos/CONVOCATORIA%2028ed.pdf)
- **27th edition details on follow-up support:**
  - Entrepreneurship top 3 were invited by IEEGL to "recibir orientación y acompañamiento para explorar el potencial emprendedor de su proyecto… con miras a su posible validación y evolución como proyecto emprendedor".
  - Social Impact top 3 were invited by DSS to "asesoría personalizada y acompañamiento… por medio de vinculaciones con organizaciones, y/o a través de la experiencia de Proyecto Solidarios de servicio social a lo largo de un año".
  — [Conv27](https://expoingenierias-mty.github.io/pagina-inicio/documentos/CONVOCATORIA%2027ed.pdf)
- **Entrepreneurship rubric (Annex 7), /10 points.** Five criteria, each scored 0 (Deficiente), 1 (En desarrollo) or 2 (Consolidado):
  1. Problem or opportunity identified (a real one).
  2. Market or potential short-term users.
  3. Value proposition and differentiation vs existing solutions, with technology enabling an innovation "con potencial de crecimiento".
  4. Commercialization potential (how it becomes a product or service).
  5. Technical evaluation. Academic score of 1–33% earns 0 points, 34–66% earns 1, and 67–100% earns 2.
  Teams must answer three questions at registration and in the video: real problem, short-term users, and whether it could be sold. — [Conv28](https://expoingenierias-mty.github.io/pagina-inicio/documentos/CONVOCATORIA%2028ed.pdf)
- **Social Impact rubric (Annex 8), /10 points.** Five criteria, each scored 0/1/2:
  1. **Concreto:** "producto tangible que se puede entregar física o digitalmente". A product that is unfinished or still in development scores lower.
  2. **Solidario:** "recibido por un usuario o beneficiario directo de algún sector en estado de vulnerabilidad, quien también participa del diseño de la solución". A score of 0 applies if the user "no participa del diseño… o no lo recibió".
  3. **De impacto social:** to score 2, "se presenta una medición del impacto… bajo criterios, indicadores e instrumentos de medición claros y se identifica claramente a qué Objetivo(s) de Desarrollo Sostenible se impacta". A score of 1 applies if impact is communicated without criteria-based measurement.
  4. **Formativo:** applies transversal and disciplinary competencies.
  5. **Technical evaluation**, using the same academic-score bands as above.
  — [Conv28](https://expoingenierias-mty.github.io/pagina-inicio/documentos/CONVOCATORIA%2028ed.pdf)
- Registration and video requirements for Social Impact:
  - (a) The SDG(s) addressed, for a "diagnosed" social problem.
  - (b) "Vinculación con el entorno": which vulnerable group benefits and how it takes part in the design, plus "qué actores sociales apoyaron o contribuyeron en la investigación, diseño y validación del prototipo".
  - (c) "Compromiso ciudadano": ethical, sustainable and solidarity-based solutions that promote collaboration.
  — [Conv28](https://expoingenierias-mty.github.io/pagina-inicio/documentos/CONVOCATORIA%2028ed.pdf)
- **Historical prizes:**
  - Conexión Tec 2019 (Monterrey): winners in the "Productos o servicios para emprendimiento de base tecnológica" category got a one-semester scholarship at the Incubadora de Empresas del Tec. — [Conecta Tec, May 2019](https://conecta.tec.mx/es/noticias/monterrey/educacion/premia-tec-la-innovacion-y-el-emprendimiento-en-la-ingenieria)
  - Guadalajara 2020: the Entrepreneurship Department's top 3 got an incubation scholarship. — [Conecta Tec GDL, Dec 2020](https://conecta.tec.mx/es/noticias/guadalajara/educacion/asi-es-expo-ingenierias-del-tec-soluciones-innovadoras-y)
  - A search snippet (article not opened) says a Monterrey edition (~19th, ~2022) offered Tec Lean workshop scholarships from Zona Ei and a chance to join INC Monterrey 2022. — [Conecta Tec, "Conectarán los mejores proyectos…"](https://conecta.tec.mx/es/noticias/monterrey/educacion/conectaran-los-mejores-proyectos-de-ingenieria-en-region-monterrey) (unverified)
- **Hidalgo 2025** jury: professors and EXATEC members. — [Conecta Tec Hidalgo, Dec 2025](https://conecta.tec.mx/es/noticias/hidalgo/educacion/retos-reales-estudiantes-presentan-soluciones-en-expo-ingenierias)

### Inferences
- A single project can compete for **three separate recognitions**: its category placing, Social Impact top 3 and Entrepreneurship top 3. The academic score feeds 2 of the 10 points in each attribute rubric. Strong technical execution therefore counts toward every award.
- The Social Impact rubric is very specific and reachable. The team needs (1) a delivered, tangible artifact or digital tool, (2) **real low-resource banana growers who took part in the design** (interviews, field tests, co-design sessions, with evidence), (3) **measured impact with named indicators and instruments** plus SDG mapping, and (4) evidence of competencies. Without real community participation, "Solidario" scores 0–1 and "Concreto" and "De impacto social" weaken. This is the single biggest lever for a community-focused refocus.
- The DSS follow-up offered to Social Impact winners (up to a year of support or links with organizations) fits a team that wants to keep working with banana communities after the course.

### Gaps
- What the "premio en especie" and the IEEGL "premio especial" are for AD2026 is not specified.
- Judge names are not published for any edition I found.
- I could not confirm whether Guadalajara or Occidente uses the same three-jury structure and rubrics in 2026. The Monterrey rubrics are the closest published evidence.

## Q3. Past winners or featured projects and their characteristics

### Takeaway

## Q4. Comparable competitions: what wins them

### Takeaway
Across competitions the same pattern repeats. Evidence of **validation with real users or customers**, a **working and tested prototype**, a clear **problem → solution → measured impact** chain, and a team that **answers questions with depth** beat raw technical novelty. The ISEF engineering rubric puts 35% of the score on presentation and interview and 20% on tested execution. Imagine Cup judges score customer validation, technical excellence and viability. Ag-robotics prizes for drones went to solutions tied to specific crops and farm operations.

### Cited Findings
- **ISEF (Society for Science), engineering projects, 100 points.** I. Research Problem 10 (practical need, solution criteria, constraints). II. Design and Methodology 15 (alternatives explored, prototype). III. Execution: Construction and Testing **20** ("prototype has been tested in multiple conditions/trials"; "demonstrates engineering skill and completeness"). IV. Creativity & Potential Impact **20**. V. Presentation **35**, split into poster 10 and interview **25**. Interview items:
  - "clear, concise, thoughtful responses to questions"
  - "understanding interpretation and limitations of results and conclusions"
  - "recognition of potential impact in science, society and/or economics"
  - "quality of ideas for further research"
  - "for team projects, contributions to and understanding of project by all members"
  — [Society for Science, ISEF Grand Award Criteria](https://www.societyforscience.org/isef/grand-award/criteria/)
- **Microsoft Imagine Cup.** A FAQ page (staging domain, apparently the 2027 season, deadline 28 Feb 2027) lists the scoring stages:
  - Application review: startup profile, product/technical approach, problem/market opportunity, team execution potential.
  - Expert review: business readiness, technical excellence, customer value.
  - Final judging: **"Enterprise customer validation, investability, technical excellence, and customer value."**
  Required materials are a pitch deck, a 2–3 minute demo video, an architecture diagram, and a live demo and pitch in the interview. No weights are published. — [Imagine Cup FAQ (staging)](https://imaginecup-stg.microsoft.com/pl-pl/support/faq)
- Imagine Cup 2026 world finalists: CopyFlag (UK), Revora Health (US) and SpoilSafe (US, low-cost gas sensors plus ML for food freshness). Microsoft said the differentiator was "how these startups built their solutions—not just what they built", with founders showing clear understanding of the problem and where to take it next. — [Microsoft Tech Community, 2026 finalists](https://techcommunity.microsoft.com/blog/studentdeveloperblog/introducing-the-2026-imagine-cup-world-finalists/4509670)

### Inferences
- Common traits of winners across these sources:
  - (a) A specific beneficiary group and a number reached (OAKs: 500+ farmers).
  - (b) A prototype tested under multiple conditions (ISEF III).
  - (c) Validation with real users or customers (Imagine Cup final criterion).
  - (d) A low-cost angle for resource-constrained users (SpoilSafe's "low-cost sensors", OAKs).
  - (e) A team where every member can explain the system and its limitations (ISEF interview).
- The OAKs Filtration result is the closest analogue: a Tec mechatronics student, agricultural users, and impact quantified in farmers. It shows Tec's own social-innovation juries reward "number of producers reached + concrete agricultural problem". Entering PEGS Innovación Social Estudiantil is a natural later step after Expo.
- Drone crop-disease detection is a recurring student theme worldwide. The novelty claim should therefore rest on the **offline, low-cost, community-deployable** design for banana smallholders and on field evidence, not on "drone + AI detects disease" alone.

## Q5. Practical stand and presentation best practices for engineering expos

### Takeaway
The Expo Ingenierías rules imply a few practical requirements. The prototype must be shown working "robustly and consistently". The video and profile have strict formats where breaking a rule means disqualification. Attribute juries score in person only. General competition evidence (ISEF) says the interview carries the most points, so every team member must handle questions on limitations and impact.

### Cited Findings
- Physical prototypes must demonstrate "que tu prototipo funciona de manera robusta y consistente —el mismo desempeño siempre—" (video requirement). The academic criterion asks: "¿Se aprecia que el prototipo resiste y funciona consistentemente?" — [Conv28](https://expoingenierias-mty.github.io/pagina-inicio/documentos/CONVOCATORIA%2028ed.pdf)
- The in-person contest runs 12:00–5:00 pm at Arena Borregos and is open to the public. Entrepreneurship and Social Impact juries evaluate only in person. — [Conv28](https://expoingenierias-mty.github.io/pagina-inicio/documentos/CONVOCATORIA%2028ed.pdf)
- The video must not be fully AI-generated and must mix team explanation with footage of tests and results, supported by "figuras, diagramas o gráficas". — [Conv28](https://expoingenierias-mty.github.io/pagina-inicio/documentos/CONVOCATORIA%2028ed.pdf)
- Profile images must show the project, not team members. An animated GIF is recommended as the first image. — [Conv28](https://expoingenierias-mty.github.io/pagina-inicio/documentos/CONVOCATORIA%2028ed.pdf)
- ISEF: the interview is worth 25/100, covering "understanding interpretation and limitations of results", "quality of ideas for further research", and every member's understanding. The poster is worth 10/100: "logical organization… clarity of graphics and legends… supporting documentation displayed". — [Society for Science](https://www.societyforscience.org/isef/grand-award/criteria/)
- Occidente 2021 used a "pitch de venta" before a jury. The GDL mechatronics director said students must "present and sell" their projects. — [Conecta Tec Morelia](https://conecta.tec.mx/es/noticias/morelia/educacion/un-mejor-espacio-estudiantes-de-campus-morelia-destacan-por-proyecto); [Conecta Tec GDL, Jan 2019](https://conecta.tec.mx/es/noticias/guadalajara/educacion/proyectos-de-ingenieria-de-alumnos-del-tec-resuelven-problemas)
- Imagine Cup final judging includes a live demo and pitch in the interview, on top of a 2–3 minute recorded demo. — [Imagine Cup FAQ (staging)](https://imaginecup-stg.microsoft.com/pl-pl/support/faq)

### Inferences (general advice, not official Expo rules)
- **Demo reliability:** Flying a drone inside Arena Borregos is probably not possible or allowed (unconfirmed, ask organizers). Plan a static, fail-safe demo instead:
  - Drone on the table with the companion computer running live inference on real or printed Sigatoka leaf samples.
  - The offline web app on a laptop showing a georeferenced map from a recorded field flight.
  - A looping field-flight video as backup.
  - Run the "same input → same output" test repeatedly in front of judges to satisfy the robustness criterion.
- **Evidence wall for the Social Impact jury:** photos and quotes or testimonials from participating growers, a co-design log (dates, number of producers, feedback incorporated), indicators with baseline and target (for example, detection accuracy in the field, time to scout one hectare vs manual, cost per hectare, number of producers trained), and SDG icons (likely SDG 2, plus 1/8/12/15 as fits).
- **Entrepreneurship jury:** a one-slide unit-economics/cost breakdown (bill of materials vs commercial alternatives), the target user and channel (cooperative or extension service), and the differentiator ("offline, low-cost, community-operated").
- **Pitch:** a 60–90 second version (problem with a number → who suffers → demo → measured result → impact → ask) and a 3–5 minute version that mirrors the video structure the call requires. Every member should rehearse answers on model limitations (false positives, lighting, disease stage), dataset provenance, and next steps.
- Keep the 90-character project name, the 30-character filenames and the 3-image profile exactly compliant. These are easy disqualifiers.

### Gaps
- No official Expo Ingenierías guidance on stand layout, poster size, power, or whether a printed poster is required.
- No published judge feedback explaining why specific Expo Ingenierías projects won.
